ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Making Health Monitoring Simpler

SmartHealth is a web-based health monitoring system that helps patients record and manage their health information. It provides an easy-to-use dashboard to track health readings, view trends, and receive rule-based alerts for better health awareness.
